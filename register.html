CYF Event Rules & Regulations

By participating in this event, you agree to follow the rules and guidelines established by the CYF organizers.

  • Respect all event organizers, staff, chaperones, speakers, and fellow participants.
  • Follow the event schedule and instructions.
  • Maintain appropriate behavior throughout the event.
  • Respect the venue, facilities, and other property.
  • Follow all safety instructions.
  • Avoid activities that may endanger yourself or other participants.
  • Notify event staff immediately if an emergency occurs.
  • Participants are expected to uphold Christian values and conduct.
