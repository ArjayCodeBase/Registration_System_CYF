Privacy Policy

How the CYF Registration System collects, uses, protects, and manages information.

Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the CYF Registration System handles information submitted through this website.

📝

Information We Collect

When you register for a CYF event, become a sponsor, contact us, or use other features of the website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

This may include your name, contact information, registration information, participant details, event information, sponsorship information, and other information required to process your request.

⚙️

How We Use Information

Information submitted through the system may be used to process event registrations, manage participants, coordinate sponsorships, process related requests, and provide event-related communication.

We may also use the information to maintain accurate registration records and improve the operation of the registration system.

✉️

Email Communication

The system may send registration confirmations, payment notifications, event-related updates, and other administrative messages by email.

Email services may be provided through authorized third-party services used by the CYF Registration System.

💳

Payments

Payments made through the system may be processed by third-party payment providers. Payment information is handled according to the policies and security practices of the applicable payment provider.

The CYF Registration System does not intend to store complete payment card numbers, card security codes, or other sensitive payment credentials on its own servers.

🔗

Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with authorized individuals, service providers, or systems when reasonably necessary to operate the registration system, process payments, send communications, or provide requested services.

🔒

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information submitted through the system.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

🗃️

Data Retention

Registration and related information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to administer CYF events, maintain organizational records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable obligations.

🌐

Third-Party Services

The system may use third-party services for hosting, payment processing, email delivery, authentication, analytics, or other technical functions.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

👤

Your Choices

If you have questions about information submitted through the registration system, or believe information associated with your registration needs to be corrected, you may contact the CYF team.

Requests may be reviewed and handled according to applicable requirements and operational needs.

🛡️

Minors and Participants

Some CYF activities may involve youth participants. Where required, registration and participation information should be provided or authorized by a parent, guardian, or other appropriate responsible person.

🔄

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the registration system, services, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page.

📩

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or information handled by the CYF Registration System, please contact the CYF team through the Contact page.
